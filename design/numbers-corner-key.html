The corner key

A mock for you to tap, not the app. Chat ruled one new key in the keypad's empty bottom-left corner: a comma in the price sessions and a colon in The hour. The keys and colours below are the live ones. Both pads work: tap the answer in.

2. How it behaves

  • Same pad, same places. The digits never move. The new key takes the one empty corner, and that corner is free on every number screen you have today.
  • No Enter key. It finishes by itself when the last digit is right, as the number screens do now.
  • A wrong tap shakes red and does not go in, as now.
  • Prices: a whole price takes no comma (treinta y cinco is 3, 5). With cents it is always the comma and two digits.
  • The hour: always the hour, the colon, and two digits. "A las nueve" is 9:00.
  • When it lands, the words and the number show together, with the euro sign on a price.
