## 1. THE TOP ITEM: THE REVIEW ROOM HAS NO DOOR

The constitution maps two tracks: Learn acquires, Scenes compose, and the six-week tilt moves the learner between them. Three weeks of building produced four scenes, the circuit, the beat family, and the cast. **A learner cannot reach any of it.** Scenes serve only from the Test Lab; the Practice door serves circuits by due-mass but nothing announces scenes exist; the tilt (build-order step 5) was never wired; the Learn tab still presents chapters as the product.

Every other friction on this map is felt inside a session. This one is felt as "the journey is unclear," because the map has a second track and the road does not.

**Build (Code, one increment):** a Scenes entry in the Practice sheet when a scene's due-mass clears the threshold (and the review tile naming it: "Scene: Café Paloma is ready"), plus the tilt: the review room's share of the daily mix grows with the countdown [tune]. No new design needed for v1; the scene door exists.

## 3. THE LENGTH INCONSISTENCY (measured, not felt)

Three session kinds were sized in three sessions against three rulers:
- **Machine lessons**: measured and sized to ~4.4 real minutes (depth ruling 2, v234).
- **Ladder lessons**: ~29 beats composed; at Tom's medians that is roughly 4-5 minutes, but never re-measured after the depth changes. Note the composer's own mix: present 21%, type-it-cold 22%, the encore lap 15%. The lap alone is a sixth of a lesson.
- **Circuits**: 8 phrases × ~3 forms = 23 reps, ~3-4 minutes.
- **Scenes**: 6-9 asks (~2-3 minutes); Tom called The Corner and Toni's short (9/7-6, 9/7-7).

Verdict needed: one target band for a session regardless of kind (the atlas already scores in real minutes), and scenes sized up to it by adding beats/overheards, not prose.

## 5. THE PROCESS FIX: "SURFACES TOUCHED"

The two-door problem, the translate-tap overlap (fixed 9/7-1a), and the chain-vs-scene duplicate all came from the same cause: a session ruled a new thing well without mapping what it touched. From now on:
- Every chat handoff carries a **Surfaces touched** line before the build: which existing screens this changes, duplicates, or makes inconsistent.
- Every Code ship report carries the same line after the build.
- One surface open at a time, in the order below; batches go to the parking lot; "parked, not this track" is a normal answer.
