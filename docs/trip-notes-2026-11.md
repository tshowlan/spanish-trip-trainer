## What the notes say together

- **The table is where the trip happened.** Fourteen of the thirty are at a table (batches 3, 4, 5, and half of 6), and the Table session teaches arriving and ordering for one. It has nothing for a group, nothing for asking again, and nothing for the meal's end.
- **The Street was written for a traveler without a phone.** Five notes say the same thing from different sides: nobody asks strangers how far the station is. The phone answers where; the traveler needs "here" and a way to confirm.
- **Paying has a step the app skips:** finding out where to pay, and the question that comes with the card machine.
- **Barcelona speaks two languages on its signs.** The app's Spain is Castilian only.
