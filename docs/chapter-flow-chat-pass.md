## RULINGS

- **The floor:** chapter 1's laps stay on tiles. Cold typing enters with chapter 2 (sentences by place), in the lap first, per ruling 6. Chunks are being met in chapter 1; meeting and cold don't share a chapter.
- **The chunk session:** one session of eight (Code's lean confirmed). Eight cards → the words board → eight rungs → the listening lap sits inside the band; two sessions of four would be thin.
- **Chapter titles (naming law, plain English):** rendered as Chapter 1-4 even if the engine counts from 0 — "Chapter 0" reads like a bug to a learner. **1 · The words** · **2 · Your first sentences** · **3 · Sentences by place** · **4 · Conversations**.
- **The glue allowance (Tom, ratified):** GLUE may ride inside a context from the session where a pair or a noun introduces it (o via ¿tarjeta o efectivo?; un/una via the first counter nouns; the article that belongs to an item, as in la cuenta; de via de nada) — glossed by tap, never an item, never the word under test.
- **"Already met" means the session's cards plus prior sessions (Tom's correction):** the cards present every word before the board and the rungs, so any context may use any word from this session or an earlier one; only the target word is under test. Card order carries no constraint, and there is no first-word problem.

## THE EIGHT ASKS (chapter 1, session 1 — items with glosses for the fused reveal)

| Chunk | Meaning | Word glosses |
|---|---|---|
| Quiero ___ | I want ___ | quiero = I want |
| ¿Me puede traer ___? | Could you bring me ___? | me = to me · puede = could you · traer = bring |
| Necesito ___ | I need ___ | necesito = I need |
| Tengo alergia a ___ | I'm allergic to ___ | tengo = I have · alergia = allergy · a = to |
| ¿Dónde está ___? | Where is ___? | dónde = where · está = is |
| ¿Hay ___? | Is there ___? | hay = is there / are there |
| ¿Cuánto cuesta ___? | How much is ___? | cuánto = how much · cuesta = it costs |
| ¿A qué hora ___? | What time ___? | a qué hora = at what time |

"Which one do you say?" situations (one line each, English, voice-lawful): the waiter passes and you want the check (Bring) · the glasses are empty (Bring) · you're at the counter and know what you want (Want) · your phone is at 2% (Need) · before the tapas arrive, the shellfish (Allergy, straight) · two unmarked doors (Find) · the room looks full (There) · the tag is missing (Price) · the gate is down (When).
