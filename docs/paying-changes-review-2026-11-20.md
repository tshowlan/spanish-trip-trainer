## 1. The card: ¿Pago aquí?

| | Was | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Example on the card | ¿Pago aquí o en la barra? (Do I pay here or at the bar?) | **¿Pago aquí o en caja? (Do I pay here or at the register?)** |
| Good to know | Pagar is to pay, and pago is I pay. Two words, and the question is in your voice. | **Aquí is wherever you are when you ask: from your table, it means the table. The other answer is en caja, at the register.** |

## 2. The sentence: ¿Pago aquí?

| | Was | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Wrong answers offered | Can I pay here? · Do I pay at the bar? | **Do I pay now? · Do I pay at the register?** |
| You hear back | Sí, aquí (Yes, here) · No, en la barra (No, at the bar) · En caja (At the register) | Sí, aquí (Yes, here) · **No, en caja (No, at the register)** · **En la barra (At the bar)** |

## 3. The second sentence: you now say the register

| | Was | Now |
|---|---|---|
| You say | ¿Pago aquí o en la barra? | **¿Pago aquí o en caja?** |
| English | Do I pay here or at the bar? | **Do I pay here or at the register?** |
| Built from | Do I pay = pago · here = aquí · or = o · at the bar? = en la barra | Do I pay = pago · here = aquí · or = o · **at the register? = en caja** |
| Wrong answers offered | Do I pay here or at the register? · Can I pay by card at the bar? | **Do I pay here or at the bar? · Can I pay by card at the register?** |
| You hear back | Aquí mismo (Right here) · En la barra (At the bar) · Donde quiera (Wherever you like) | **En caja, por favor (At the register, please)** · Aquí mismo (Right here) · Donde quiera (Wherever you like) |

So: you say **caja**, and **la barra** is only something you hear.

## 4. Two price lines out, the total in
