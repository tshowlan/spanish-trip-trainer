## 1. The ¿Lleva ___? card: rewritten by chat

Chat's reason: "Does it have meat?" against "Is it vegetarian?" is one question asked twice. The mix-up worth heading off is lleva (does it have) with tiene (do you have).

| Card | Right meaning | Was | Now |
|---|---|---|---|
| ¿Lleva carne? | Does it have meat? | Is it vegetarian? | **Do you have meat?** |
| ¿Lleva gluten? | Does it have gluten? | (the nearest card in the session) | **Do you have gluten?** |
| ¿Lleva marisco?, and each other allergy | Does it have shellfish? | (the nearest card in the session) | **Do you have shellfish?** (its own word each time) |

## 5. Every line of Table sentences · Part 2 now plays the first time

A play takes three sentences of each kind. Four sat under "another", so one always waited. "¿Me puede traer la carta otra vez?" has moved to the plain ask (otra vez means again; nothing is being replaced). Part 2 now holds three under another (fork, glass, bottle) and three under the ask (plate, check, menu again).

## 6. Sessions that still hold more than three of one kind (for chat to retag or trim)

Chat's new rule: no more than three lines of one kind in a session, so one play reaches all of them. Today's breaks:

- **Help sentences**, the "necesito" lines: 4 for everyone.
  - Necesito ayuda, por favor. · Necesito un médico. Urgente. · Necesito la policía. · Necesito un hospital, por favor.
- **Counter sentences**, the "tiene" lines: 4 for celiac, up to 7 for vegetarian, lactose-free, allergy to dairy, allergy to gluten.
  - ¿Tiene algo sin gluten? · ¿Tiene algo sin lactosa? · ¿Tiene algo vegetariano? · ¿Tiene leche sin lactosa? · ¿Tiene cerveza sin gluten? · ¿Tiene té con leche? · ¿Tiene agua sin gas?
- **Your table lines**, the "hay" lines: 4 for vegetarian, lactose-free, allergy to dairy, allergy to gluten.
  - ¿Hay algo sin gluten en la carta? · ¿Hay algo sin lactosa en la carta? · ¿Hay algo vegetariano en la carta? · ¿Hay contaminación cruzada?

The Counter and Your table lines only break the rule for learners with dietary answers: each answer adds its own lines to the two everyone gets.
