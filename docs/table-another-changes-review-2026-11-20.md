## 4. Four new sentences: asking for another

Each is built from "Could you = puede · bring = traer · me = me" plus the pieces shown.

| You say | New pieces | Wrong answers offered | You hear back |
|---|---|---|---|
| **¿Me puede traer otro tenedor?** (Could you bring me another fork?) | another fork? = otro tenedor | Could you bring me another knife? · Could you bring me a plate? | Vale, uno limpio (Okay, a clean one) · ¿Se ha caído? (Did it fall?) · Un momento (One moment) |
| **¿Me puede traer otro vaso?** (Could you bring me another glass?) | another glass? = otro vaso | Could you bring me another bottle? · Could you bring me a glass of wine? | Vale, ¿para el agua? (Okay, for the water?) · ¿Con hielo? (With ice?) · Ahora mismo (Right away) |
| **¿Me puede traer otra botella de agua?** (Could you bring me another bottle of water?) | another bottle = otra botella · of water? = de agua | Could you bring me another glass of water? · Could you bring me a bottle of wine? | ¿Con gas o sin gas? (Sparkling or still?) · ¿Grande o pequeña? (Large or small?) · Vale (Okay) |
| **¿Me puede traer la carta otra vez?** (Could you bring me the menu again?) | the menu = la carta · again? = otra vez | Could you bring me the check? · Could you bring me another menu? | ¿Para el postre? (For dessert?) · Aquí tiene (Here you go) · Vale (Okay) |
