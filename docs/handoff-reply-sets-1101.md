## PART 1 — RULINGS

**1. The ceiling, ratified with a floor.** A stock reply is HEARD at most once per place where it is the natural answer, with two exceptions at the Counter, where the learner is newest: Un momento, Ahora mismo, and ¿Algo más? may each be heard twice there. Everywhere else a stock reply rides only as one of the three meanings offered. The engine's rarest-first rule stays, with the floor made explicit: **each stock reply present in a place is heard once there** (the first sentence carrying it), otherwise rarest-first would bury the stock replies entirely now that nearly every sentence has two of its own.

**2. Replies become counted things, lightly.** Yes, a reply gets a record and a strength, because the constitution says understanding the reply matters as much as producing the ask, and the retired session did track them. But a reply is a **hear item**: it never produces, it never gets its own rung, and its strength moves only when an exchange that carries it is picked right or wrong (the sentence and the reply both record). It counts in the Progress tab's wins as "replies you can hear" (and its own line in the ceremony's ledger: "3 replies heard for the first time"). A weak reply is served through the sentences that carry it: rarest-first with a weakness tiebreak. No drills, no cards, no review door for replies. This is a build; nothing writes until Code sizes it.

**3. Code's deviation on ¿Puedo pagar con tarjeta?: accepted.** One item, one session, the Counter. The Shop gets **"¿Puedo pagar con tarjeta aquí?"** (aquí on the hint; replies Sí, claro · Solo efectivo · Mínimo diez (h)).

**4. The Shop's ¿Tiene chocolate sin gluten? variants:** dairy, nuts, peanuts, egg, and soy apply (they hide in chocolate); shellfish and fish do not, and those learners get the gluten base. The general rule for the generator: a variant applies when the allergen plausibly hides in the noun.

**5. la estación = "the train station"** everywhere, chapter 1's card included.

**6. The taxi asks, re-homed.** "Necesito un taxi, por favor." goes to the **Table** (after the meal, to the waiter; replies Ahora mismo · Cinco minutos · ¿Para cuántos?). "Necesito un taxi al hotel." waits for the hotel-desk session in chapter 3. The Street gains the driver's own line, no verb, like Para mí: **"Al hotel, por favor."** and **"A la estación, por favor."** (frame tag `a`; replies Muy bien · ¿Cuál? (h, Which one?) · Son veinte / Muy bien · ¿La de tren? (h) · Son diez). Street is thirteen.

**7. The intake and Known.** The level quiz retires: it placed nothing after 10/31 and a quiz that informs nothing is friction. In its place, one screen, voice-lawful: **"Already know some Spanish?"** / "Swipe any session on the Learn tab and mark it Known. It will not be taught or reviewed. Remove Known brings it back." / one button, "Got it". No list of chapter 1's sessions in the intake: the Learn tab is that list, and marking there is the one way, not two. The row's two swipe actions: Known on the right (gold, the check), Forget on the left (the dim grey of faded); never both on one side.

**8. The earned state: SOLID, not settled.** "Settled" sounds permanent; the state can fade, and the app already speaks of strength as holding / slipping / faded. **Solid** is the word: it is a strength word, it is honest ("solid for now"), and it reads in the whisper: **"Counter words · Part 1 is solid."** The bar: every word in the session in the top band (80) at once AND still there at its next review, so one lucky pass does not light it [tune]. It fades quietly when a word lapses, no toast, no loss line. Same check as Known; the K stays on declared ones. **A whole chapter going solid gets the ceremony's light, once** ("Chapter 1 is solid."): it is mechanical and true, so it passes the decay test. Belongs with the Progress tab session; nothing builds until then except the state itself.
