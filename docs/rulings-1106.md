### 2. Where the cards live after the first meeting

**(a) The surface: the Learn row opens to its cards.** Code's view confirmed; no new screen, and it serves the Known learner, who otherwise never meets a card.

- **The door:** the ring at the row's right edge (the session's state) opens the session's cards; the row body still plays. The ring is the state, and the cards are what the state is made of, so the ring is the honest door. (A long press is invisible; a third control on the row is clutter.) The hit area is the full row height.
- **The sheet:** title is the session's name. One line under it: **"The cards, as you met them."** For a Known session: **"The cards. Marked Known, so they are not taught or reviewed."**
- **Each card, read-only, in the card's own dress:** the Spanish headword, its gloss, the context line in Spanish with the English under it, and the Good to know note where the card had one. A tap on the Spanish says it. No strength, no doors, no buttons. Frame cards (¿Me cobra?, Al hotel, Está bien así) are listed like any other.
- **Not here:** sentences. They are not cards, and the Stats place sheet already lists them with their strength.
- **No in-session door.** The cards are a thing you read, not a thing you do, and the session is for doing.

**(b) Place sessions' frame cards follow the once rule.** A frame card shows the first time the session is played and not on replay, like a word session's cards; the replay opens on the frame's build. Two things keep the card from being lost: the Learn sheet above, and the Good to know note's return on every second miss in a row (Tom's rule). The one exception already built stands: cards for words or frames gained since the last play.
