## B. RECOMMENDED, FOR TOM

### B1. Scenes become the form review takes, not a chapter and not a second track

Tom's lean (scenes as dynamic review; chapter 4 not only scenes) is the stronger design, for three reasons the last month supplied:

1. **A written scene cannot use what the learner needs today.** Its lines are pinned. Review knows which lines are due; a scene that is composed can be made of exactly those.
2. **The order already exists.** Batch 4 cut the Table along the meal. A place's lines now have a sequence (arrive, order, mid-meal, the end, pay), so the composer can run a learner's due lines as one visit, each waiter's reply leading to the next line, instead of twenty unrelated cards.
3. **It is where "conversation" comes from.** A conversation is asks and answers in a row. No chapter has to teach that as content; a visit that strings met lines together is it, and it lengthens by itself as the chapters add turns.

So "review by place" grows into **a visit**: a place, a moment of the day, six to ten of the learner's own due lines in the order they happen, each answer still recorded on its own item.

### B2. Situations, not stories: the learner is the only character

Agreed with Tom's lean away from character-led storylines, and here is what that keeps and what it gives up.

- **Kept:** everything in the scene laws that is craft and not cast. The place and the time of day, the one-line beats, the stanza form, the overheard line, the narrative italic, the End Scene label, the door.
- **Given up:** named, recurring people (Marina, Toni, Rosa, Andrés, Pau, Montse) and the arcs between them; "cast met first"; first names. The other speaker is a role: the waiter, the barman, the driver.
- **What replaces character is the learner's own trip.** The visit knows the destination, the dates, the Barcelona layer, the dietary lines, who they order for. That is more personal than a cast, it costs nothing per country, and it is the same instinct as Tom's "where your personality comes out."
- **The five written scenes** stay as they are until composed visits can replace them; no new cast scenes are written. Andrés' taxi, still with Tom, is released.

The risk is real and should be named: without a cast, a visit can go dry and read as a drill in a costume. The guard is the beats. One sensory line between asks, in the second person, is what makes it a rehearsal of a meal and not a quiz about one.

- **Why answering comes before the personal:** in thirty trip notes and the play since, being asked a question and having to answer it came up about three times as often as wanting to say something about himself. And it is still need: "¿Con gas o sin gas?" stands between you and the water.

In chapter 3 they answer it. One new exercise (hear the question, build the answer), no new places, a handful of sessions by place.
- **Chapter 4 is where answers stop being a choice of three**: preferences, opinions, a kind no, a compliment to the kitchen, a word about yourself. It is the first chapter beyond the plan in spirit as well as position.
