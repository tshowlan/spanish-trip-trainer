# Chapter 2, "Your first sentences": the sessions (redrawn 2026-10-24)

Eight ways to ask, then the asks filled with the words you own, one place at a time. Eight sessions; two live, one staged.

1. **The asks · Part 1** (LIVE). Quiero, ¿Me puede traer?, ¿Dónde está?, ¿Cuánto cuesta?.
2. **The asks · Part 2** (LIVE). Necesito, ¿Hay?, ¿Tiene?, ¿Puedo?.
3. **Counter sentences** (STAGED sentences-1). Coffee, beer, water and the card at the counter: seven sentences built from chapter 1's words, each built from tiles, heard, answered (the exchange), then the board.
4. **Table sentences.** The table, the menu, the check, bread, water, allergies; Quiero una mesa para dos.
5. **Paying sentences.** ¿Cuánto cuesta esto?, card or cash, change, the check; the prices in the replies once the hour and the numbers machine exist.
6. **Street sentences.** Where is, is there one nearby, is it far, I need a taxi.
7. **Shop sentences.** Do you have, can I try it, is it open, which day (the days and the signs from chapter 1).
8. **Help sentences.** I need help, a doctor, the hospital, No entiendo, ¿Habla inglés?.
9. **Numbermachine.** Numbers inside the sentences: prices and the hour; ¿A qué hora? and its replies.

Retiring as the places land: the four old rooms, and What they say back as a session (its beat is the exchange inside each place). Review carries the chapter: a due ask comes back as a situation and its sentence (live), and a solid word comes back inside a sentence that carries it (the review refit, next).

**Kept in mind for later (10/25): ¿Qué?** is not an ask until its replies are learnable. First seat: ¿A qué hora? in Numbermachine. After that only with a pointable answer (¿Qué es esto?, ¿Qué me recomienda?) in a later place session, once food and dish words exist to be the answers. See the ledger, 10/25.
