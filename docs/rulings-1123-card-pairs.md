## A. THE RULE: what makes a good wrong meaning on a card

Code's fix is right, and fixed is right: an introduction is never a test (10/04), so the pair is part of the card, not a quiz. Its job is to make the learner read the meaning by setting it against the one thing it could be mistaken for.

1. **One step away.** The opposite, the pair, or the thing an English speaker really confuses it with (la carta and la cuenta; hotel and hospital; pescado and marisco; hay and tiene).
2. **Clearly wrong.** Never a near-synonym, and never the same intent with the polarity flipped ("Does it have meat?" against "Is it vegetarian?" is one question asked twice).
3. **The same kind and the same shape** as the right meaning.
4. **The same every time.**
5. **Written wins; the neighbour rule is the fallback** for cards nobody has written yet, and the review list flags every fallback so it gets read.
