### 3. servicios and aseos: bathrooms and restrooms

US English has two words for the room, and they split the way Spain's do: the one you say and the one on the sign.

| word | gloss | context |
|---|---|---|
| servicios | **bathrooms** | Perdón, ¿los servicios? / Excuse me, the bathrooms? |
| aseos | **restrooms** | Aseos, a la derecha. / Restrooms, to the right. |

baño stays "bathroom" (singular, the Table's word). The GOOD TO KNOW on servicios stands as written on 11/05.
