## Doors into the app
- **Arrival (splash):** the lighthouse, the daybreak, the dials forming. Unchanged since July; loved.
- **Onboarding:** destination and date, trip kind, lodging, transport, needs and allergies, home city, the grammar question, the level check. New users only.
- **Home:** three dials, the action tile (one action: next session / review / cram) built as a button: a pressed edge, the card sinks on press, a slow gold pulse behind it, the session's one line under its title, the photo fading into a slim strip on the right that holds the chevron. The Practice row is a button the same way. The insight whisper. When a scene is ready and the trip is near, the tile reads "Scene: Café Paloma is ready." (v344)
- **Entering a session (the bloom):** the tapped tile or row breathes, a gold light rises behind it, the tile grows and lets go, the white daybreak takes over, and the session comes in on a blank screen. From the Home tile, Practice picks, and Learn rows. (v344)

- **Learn tab:** four chapters. 1 · The words that get you by (thirteen word sessions: First words 1 and 2, Words you'll hear, Numbers 1 and 2, Paying, Counter, Table, Street, Bathroom words, Signs, Help, with long sessions in parts), 2 · Your first sentences (the four rooms), 3 · Sentences by place, 4 · Conversations. A row shows its title and count; tapping it starts; the arrow on the right opens a drawer with the session's line and its words; quitting returns to Learn. The chapter heading reopens the chapter door. (v344)
- **The chapter door:** the first time a learner enters a chapter: "Chapter 1" in the logo faces, the title, the grey line ("Simple words that maximize every day of your trip."), 13 SESSIONS, Begin. Once per chapter. (v344)
- **Word sessions (chapter 1):** every word has a card with a two-to-four-word context, then the words board, one rung per word (finish the sentence / listen and build / listen and fill / build the sentence), and the listening board as the lap: the sound that plays is already selected and chains to the next, an eight-note tune climbs as pairs match, Continue in the bottom bar. One case per session. No cold typing in chapter 1. (v344)
- **Numbers:** an ear skill.
